---

## 🛰️ Project Overview

**MIN-GEO-AI** is a research-grade Earth observation and machine learning platform engineered to identify prospective manganese-bearing formations, prioritize pre-competitive exploration targets, and assist in addressing India’s critical domestic manganese production shortfalls.

The platform bridges European Space Agency (ESA Sentinel-2 MSI), USGS Landsat-9, and ISRO Cartosat Earth observation data with domain-informed structural geology and spatial machine learning (XGBoost / Random Forest).

## 🏛️ Website Structure (Multi-Page Architecture)

1. **Home (`/`)**: Hero section with remote sensing HUD telemetry, live sensor stats, and 5-step pipeline summary.
2. **Problem (`/problem`)**: Macroeconomic analysis of India's 300 MT National Steel Policy demand, domestic deficit projection (2020–2035), high-grade depletion, and greenfield drilling bottlenecks.
3. **Our Solution (`/solution`)**: Disciplined 5-stage exploration pipeline: Acquire → Process → Analyze → Predict → Validate.
4. **Remote Sensing (`/remote-sensing`)**: Multispectral band physics, interactive Sentinel-2 diagnostic spectral curves (pyrolusite vs. host rocks), DEM terrain analysis, and Earth observation satellite constellation.
5. **AI/ML Methodology (`/methodology`)**: Spatial classification architecture, spatial block cross-validation to prevent autocorrelation bias, ROC-AUC benchmarks, and interactive **Prospectivity Simulator**.
6. **Exploration Map (`/map`)**: Full-screen interactive Leaflet GIS dashboard featuring real coordinates across the Sausar, Sandur, and Gangpur fold belts, layer toggles, and feature inspection modal.
7. **Production Intelligence (`/production`)**: Connecting space triage to mining lifecycle progression (UNFC G4 → G1) and avoided capex modeling.
8. **Technology & Architecture (`/architecture`)**: 5-layer enterprise/scientific software stack diagram (Data, Processing, AI/ML, Application, Decision Layers).
9. **Research Team (`/team`)**: SIH student innovation team structure, departmental credentials, technical contributions, and academic mentors.
10. **Contact & SIH (`/contact`)**: Official problem dossier, institutional details, downloadable technical whitepaper specs, QR code, and jury query form.

## ⚖️ Geological & Scientific Compliance Disclaimer

All prospective zones, spectral ratios, and ML scores generated by this platform are for pre-competitive target screening and exploration prioritization (UNFC Category G4/G3). Remote sensing identifies surface spectral anomalies and structural traps; it does **NOT** confirm subsurface economic reserve volume or grade. All targets require mandatory ground verification, geochemical sampling, and diamond core drilling under UNFC-1997 / CRIRSCO guidelines.
